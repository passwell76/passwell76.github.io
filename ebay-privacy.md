# Privacy policy - My ebay sinc

*Last updated: 28 September 2026*

**My ebay sinc** is a personal tool used only by its owner (eBay user `passwell76`) on their own eBay seller account. It is not offered to other users.

- **What data is accessed:** only data of the owner's eBay account (listings, orders, traffic and seller performance), read on demand through official eBay APIs with the owner's consent.
- **Storage:** eBay data is not stored in any database. No data of other eBay users (such as buyers) is persisted.
- **Sharing:** data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
- **Revocation:** access can be revoked at any time from eBay account settings (Account > Permissions / third-party apps).

Contact: passwell@libero.it
